humans.json / humans.bin
  Character models and animations from "Ultimate Modular Men" and "Ultimate Modular Women" by Quaternius
  (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html, https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html)
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  16 characters (glTF "Individual Characters"); meshes welded, positions quantised, 13 animation clips per gender,
  converted by tools/humans_pack.py. Robes and head coverings (thobe, ghutra, abaya, hijab) are added in code.
